Patterns noticed

The headline. A third of 278 inventions and discoveries ended up used mainly for something else, and a fifth were lost or ignored before being rediscovered. Inventions more often end up doing something other than what they were built for than exactly what was intended, and many good ideas sat unused for decades or centuries until the surrounding economy caught up.
